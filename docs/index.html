    Settings Destinations, limits and free windows

    Fares are round trip for one traveler, from Google Flights through SerpApi. Prices include airport taxes. Bag fees and card fees are extra.

    Prices change often. Google refreshes them about once a day and the tracker checks each trip every few days (more often when it is in its best booking window), so confirm the price on the booking page before you pay.

    The tracker looks up the exact return flight only for fares under your limit, to save searches. It tries a few departure dates inside each free window, not every date. Trips that are in or near their best booking window are checked more often than ones months away.

    "Low, typical or high for this route" is Google's own rating of the fare, shown when Google provides one. "Lowest yet" means this fare beat every earlier check for the same trip by at least 3%.

    Itineraries with a layover longer than your limit (3 hours domestic, 7 hours international by default) are skipped, so the fares shown are for flights you would likely take.

    "Two one-way tickets" means buying the flight out and the flight back as separate tickets. It can cost less, but the tickets are not linked: a delay on one does not protect the other, and bags are paid per ticket.

    Airline miles are not tracked. Fare data shows the airline but not whether a fare earns miles.
